# Business Problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and current stock levels in a notebook. She needs to know how her business is performing, whether it is growing, which products need restocking, and where to focus her effort. This analysis uses core Python only to compute sales totals, averages, best/worst days, week-on-week growth, day-type classification, weekday vs weekend performance, stock reorder alerts and gross margins, and ends with actionable recommendations.

# Top three findings
1. **Week 2 grew 33.6% over Week 1** (Week 1 total KES 19,950 vs Week 2 total KES 26,650), confirming the business is on a clear upward trend.
2. **Weekends dominate sales**: average weekend day (KES 4,900) is **59.3% higher** than the average weekday (KES 3,075), so Saturday/Sunday are the critical revenue days.
3. **Two products are below reorder level** — Tomatoes (18 kg vs reorder 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg vs reorder 15 kg) — and Sukuma also has the lowest gross margin (40%), while Avocado has the highest margin (42.9%).
